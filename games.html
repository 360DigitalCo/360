More about DEGREE ratings

DEGREE is our in-house game content guide. It is not an ESRB rating.

The number is the suggested minimum age for the game's content and intensity. Each card includes a few key points explaining the rating.

7+Light arcade action, puzzles, cartoon conflict, or simple challenges.
10+More intense action, hazards, competition, or stronger themes.
13+Stronger combat, intensity, or more serious themes.
16+Heavy violence, weapon-focused gameplay, or particularly intense scenarios.

DEGREE ratings are our own guidance for 360 Games and may change when a game receives major content updates.
