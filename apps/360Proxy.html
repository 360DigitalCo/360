ℹ️ What is 360?

360 is a next-generation search engine and digital workspace. Search, chat, translate, check weather, monitor stocks, and interact with AI — all in one unified interface. Built by 360 Digital, Co.

Account

360 — Search, Chat, Maps, Mail, News & Music

360 is an all-in-one digital platform built as a privacy-respecting alternative to Google's suite of products. It provides a unified account for: web search, community chat (servers, channels, direct messages, voice calls), interactive maps, email, a news reader, and music streaming — all without advertising or data selling.

360 uses Google OAuth solely to allow users to sign in to their 360 account using their Google credentials. The application requests only the minimum scopes required to authenticate the user (email and profile). 360 does not access, read, modify, or share any Google account data beyond the sign-in identity. User data is stored on 360's own infrastructure (Supabase) and is governed by the 360 Terms of Service and Privacy Policy.

Features: web search, server-based group chat, direct messaging, voice calls, bot integration, age verification, interactive maps, email (360 Mail), news aggregation, and music playback.
