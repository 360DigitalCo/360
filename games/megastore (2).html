Open all night. Survive until 6:00 AM.

How to play

Move WASD, look mouse, sprint Shift (uses stamina), jump Space.

E searches glowing shelf tags and crates, opens doors, and hides you in warehouse lockers. F toggles the flashlight. 1 / 2 / 3 use food, battery, medkit.

Monsters see you in their line of sight and hear running, jumping and searching. Walking is quiet, standing still is quieter, and hiding makes you invisible unless they are right beside you.

A monster catch costs 50 health. At zero, it is over. The green safe room (east side) heals you and monsters cannot enter.

Weapons: left click attacks (hold it to fire automatic weapons) and the mouse wheel cycles your weapons. Keys 4 to 9 pick a slot, and pressing a slot again switches within it: 4 bat or fire axe, 5 pistol or revolver, 6 shotgun, 7 SMG or assault rifle, 8 sniper or crossbow, 9 flare gun or taser. Weapons are scattered through the store and each uses its own ammo. Loud guns draw monsters, while the crossbow and taser are nearly silent. Flares light an area and pull patrolling monsters toward them. Climbing: the shelves are 10 m tall and you can jump from plank to plank to climb them. Loot tags sit up high too, and monsters cannot climb, but a long fall hurts. Building: B places the selected piece in front of you (Q cycles barricade, iron wall, iron sheet and spike trap, R salvages). Barricades cost 2 planks and snap to a grid, so you can wall off an aisle between the big shelves. Monsters will smash them, and brutes do it fastest. Traps cost 3 planks and hurt monsters that walk over them. Planks and iron sheets turn up on shelves and crates. Iron walls are very tough and stand on the floor. Iron sheets are walkable platforms placed at your current height, so you can bridge the gap between shelves. Press the backtick key for the dev panel.

Find a key to unlock the office, where supplies are stored. Expect blackouts, alarms and surprises. It gets worse every hour.
