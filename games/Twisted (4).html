Twisted

A tornado is on the ground. You are an amateur storm chaser with a dream and a questionable old hatchback. Sell the data you collect to buy better vehicles, support trucks and upgrades. Get close enough to collect data, stay alive, and use your support vehicle. Drive with W for gas, S for brake and reverse, and A and D to steer (arrows or dragging also work). Camera: right-drag or J, L, I, K to orbit, scroll or Z and X to zoom, V to switch views (on touch, two fingers to orbit and pinch to zoom). E uses your support vehicle. Press F to get out on foot, then E places a probe by hand at full value. Press F near your vehicle to get back in. Beware: on foot, the wind will take you in a heartbeat. Fuel burns as you drive and refills in towns for cash. Weather changes over time. Kits (Kits tab) are equipment and buffs for your vehicle; equip up to 4. Press Q to photograph the storm for data (better framing and a closer storm pay more; repeat shots pay less). On foot, press E next to a placed probe to pick it up again. Controls: H horn, G headlights, Y/U signals, T hazards, N cruise, P parking brake, O switch radar site. Service your vehicle in the garage menu.
