The Long Field

Blue team vs red team on an endless airsoft field. Your flag is at the start; the red flag stands far to the north (how far depends on the map).

One BB hit and you’re out: call it, walk back, respawn. Capture the red flag by holding it for 8 seconds, and don’t let them take yours.

W A S D move   Shift sprint   C crouch   Space jump
Mouse aim, fire   Right click scope in   R reload   V auto/semi
F hold to dig   X hold to fill   G trench/dugout   Q toggle scope
1 primary   2 secondary   E swap weapon

Needs a desktop browser with a mouse. Press Esc to come back to this menu.
