Membership Status Notice

Membership roles are privileges, not permanent ownership. 360 may revoke, suspend, or modify any membership status at any time for abuse, fraud, manipulation, harassment, cheating, or other seriously inappropriate activity.

Lifetime means the membership does not expire under normal circumstances. Premium Lifetime is earned after 1,000,000 lifetime Rewards Points have been earned. It does not make the status irrevocable.

Membership may only be transferred to another account when legitimate proof of ownership or eligibility is provided and the transfer is approved.

Verified alternate accounts belonging to the same user may receive qualifying membership benefits at no additional cost. Attempts to use alternate accounts to evade restrictions can result in revocation across associated accounts.
